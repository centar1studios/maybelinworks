Project 01

Educational Publication

Fenix

Fenix is a travel-focused publication documenting kid-friendly destinations across Arizona.

The project combines editorial design, typography, photography, and travel recommendations.

One featured spread uses an Old English-inspired typeface recreated entirely by hand using sprinkles.

All design, typography, and photography were created by Maybelin Garcia Romero.

Project 02

Educational Branding

Casa Guadalupe

Casa Guadalupe is a conceptual brand identity created to build a welcoming sense of community around education.

The visual direction draws from the colors and patterns of traditional zarape textiles.

The resulting branding uses vibrant color and pattern to create a visual language rooted in education, connection, warmth, and cultural memory.

ABOUT

Maybelin Garcia Romero

The artworks I share here take me back to a time when life was simpler, and I was just beginning to discover the curious, wandering young girl within me. Growing up in LA, I was exposed to so many trends and cultures. Thanks to my parents, I visited museums, sunset beaches, amusement parks, taquerias, third spaces like public parks, and a lot of public art. My works mirror who I was, who I am, and who I want to become. This often translates into bold, unapologetic work, using my artistic voice to talk about hard things like identity and bias. I am a woman first, then an artist. This is often reflected in my typography designs, which are carefully nurtured to convey femininity. As a mom to an 18-year-old, I value education–that is evident in my work through lived experiences. I never gave myself the label of multidisciplinary artist, but as I evolve internally, I am more comfortable embracing that externally to share with others.
